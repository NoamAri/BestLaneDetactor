Lane speeds · details (intervals, timeline, method)

Space-mean speed per lane: total distance driven in the lane ÷ total time spent in it, over one stretch of road shared by all lanes of a direction (Edie's definitions; a vehicle split into two IDs or changing lanes counts exactly once). Bars: speed with its 95% interval (bootstrap over whole vehicles). A lane is announced fastest or slowest only if it leads in ≥ 90% of resamples by ≥ 10%. Graph: rolling 25 s windows; the vertical line is the video's current time.
