How it works

1 · Spot

Every vehicle, every frame

A neural network (YOLO) finds the cars, trucks and buses in each video frame. No training on this road was needed.

2 · Track

The same car over time

A tracker links the boxes into one path per vehicle, even when it hides behind a tree or a truck for a moment.

3 · Lanes

Lanes from where cars drive

Cars keep to their lanes, so their paths reveal the lanes. Lane 1 is the leftmost in the direction of travel.

4 · Metres

From pixels to km/h

The drone's wobble is removed, and pixels are turned into metres, so a far lane is not mistaken for a slow one.

5 · Verdict

Fastest only when it's real

A lane's speed is total distance ÷ total time of all its cars. A lane is called clearly fastest only with a 10% lead in 90% of resamples; otherwise it is a "close call". On MiTra this matches the official ground truth within about 3%.
